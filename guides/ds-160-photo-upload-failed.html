DS-160 Photo Upload Failed? File & Photo Checks

By the US Visa Photo developer · Reviewed November 11, 2026

Start with the actual error message and the exact file you selected. A technical upload failure, unsuitable photo and final consular decision are different problems.

What should I check first?

Check that the file is a genuine JPEG, square, between 600 and 1200 pixels per side, and 240 kB or smaller. Then review the original photo’s background, sharpness, lighting and framing. A file that passes these technical checks is not guaranteed to be accepted. Official file specifications.

On this page

  • Check the file being uploaded
  • Check the original photograph
  • An X on the confirmation page

1. Check the file being uploaded

  1. Format: use a JPEG file. Changing a filename extension does not convert HEIC or PNG content into JPEG.
  2. Pixel dimensions: width and height must match, and each must be between 600 and 1200 pixels. A rectangular original does not become square by changing a DPI setting.
  3. File size: inspect the final file, not a screenshot of it. Keep it within the official 240 kB limit.
  4. Color and compression: the guidance specifies 24-bit sRGB color and a compression ratio of no more than 20:1. Repeatedly compressing an image may make it visibly worse.
  5. Correct copy: make sure the upload picker selected your intended file rather than a different copy or a screenshot of an app screen.

Read the official digital image requirements for the complete specification.

2. Check the original photograph

Review the official photo instructions and compare the unedited original, not just a processed preview. Check that the photo is recent, sharply focused, directly facing the camera and taken against a real plain white or off-white background. If the original is unsuitable, retake it or use a professional photo service.

A resize does not repair blur, lighting or an obstructed face. Avoid retouching or digitally changing appearance to try to get past an error. Do not treat successful upload as final photo approval.

3. Is there an X instead of a photo on the confirmation page?

The Department of State DS-160 FAQ explains that an X on a DS-160 confirmation page means the photo upload failed. It instructs applicants to provide a printed photo meeting the requirements with the confirmation page and to contact the embassy or consulate for the specific procedure.

Follow the instructions of the post handling your application. This guide does not submit, amend or make a decision about your DS-160.

4. What can the app’s checklist tell you?

US Visa Photo shows file and framing information during photo preparation. Its “Ready to use” label is an app status, not a decision from the Department of State.

Check the editing limitation before paying

The current app replaces the background and has no crop-only export. It cannot certify that an image is an unedited original or that it meets every government requirement. If an unedited photo is needed, use an appropriate original and the official submission instructions.
