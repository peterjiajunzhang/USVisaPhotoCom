Photo preparation guide

DS-160 Photo Requirements: Size, Format & Editing

By the US Visa Photo developer · Reviewed November 4, 2026

A file can have the right dimensions and still be unsuitable for a visa application. Check the original photograph, file specifications and your consulate’s instructions separately.

What size is a DS-160 photo?

The Department of State’s digital image guidance specifies a square JPEG, 600–1200 pixels on each side, no larger than 240 kB. It also specifies 24-bit sRGB color and a compression ratio of no more than 20:1. These file specifications do not establish that the photographed content is acceptable. Read the official digital image requirements.

Digital file checklist

ItemOfficial digital image guidance
Shape and pixelsSquare, with width equal to height; 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels.
FormatJPEG. Renaming a HEIC or PNG file to .jpg does not convert its format.
File size240 kB or smaller. Check the file you will actually upload.
Color24-bit color in the sRGB color space.
CompressionA compression ratio no greater than 20:1; avoid a visibly degraded image.

Source: Department of State digital image requirements. Follow your application’s current instructions if they specify a particular workflow. Do not apply DS-160 guidance automatically to passport renewal or Diversity Visa entries.

What to get right when taking the photo

The official photo requirements call for a recent color photo, taken within the previous six months, with a plain white or off-white background. Face the camera directly, keep both eyes open and use a neutral expression. Review the official page for head size, clothing, head coverings and the limited exceptions for glasses.

Use even light and a real plain background when taking the photo. If the original has shadows, blur or an unsuitable background, retake it rather than trying to repair it. The Department of State recommends a professional visa photo service.

Cropping and editing limits

The Department of State says a photo must not be digitally enhanced or altered to change your appearance. Correct dimensions and a successful upload do not override that rule. Keep the original photograph, and do not assume that background replacement, retouching or AI-generated details are acceptable.

Before using this app for an application

The current US Visa Photo app crops and replaces the background. It does not offer a crop-only export. Its checklist is a preparation aid, not an official approval or a certification of an unedited original. For government submissions, start with a suitable original and follow the official photo instructions.
