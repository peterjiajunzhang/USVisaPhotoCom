US Visa Photo: Privacy, Free Preview & Export Pricing

By the US Visa Photo developer · Reviewed November 5, 2026

Know what the app does, what export costs and where its checks stop before choosing a photo or making a purchase.

Is US Visa Photo free?

Processing, preview and retries are free. Exporting one photo costs $1.99 in the U.S. App Store or U.S. Google Play, with no subscription. Your platform’s store shows the current local price before you confirm payment. App Store and Google Play purchases and export credits are separate. A free preview is not a free full-resolution export.

Does the app upload my photo?

Both apps process portraits on your device without a photo-processing upload to us. The iPhone app uses Apple’s local imaging frameworks and StoreKit for purchases. The Android app uses bundled models and Google Play purchases; Google ML Kit may send separate usage and performance diagnostics to Google. Local image processing does not mean no network activity. Read the iPhone privacy policy or the Android privacy policy for your platform.

The app can save exports in its local Saved Photos collection and offer save/share actions. Local app files are removed when you delete the app. Copies you save to the system photo library or share elsewhere are managed by those destinations, including any cloud-photo settings you have enabled. See the relevant platform policy above for saved-photo and purchase data details.

What does the current app do—and not do?

  • Photo preparation: automatic square cropping, background replacement, a preview, file/framing checks and JPEG export.
  • Editing: the current app replaces the background; it does not provide a crop-only export or manual photo editing.
  • Lighting: it can warn about uneven facial lighting; it does not provide the planned face-lighting correction.
  • Printing: it exports an image; a multi-photo 4 × 6 print sheet is not a released feature.
  • Application handling: it does not upload or submit a visa application, and its checklist is not an official acceptance decision.
Before using a photo for a government application

The Department of State requires an appropriate original photo and prohibits digital changes to appearance. Background replacement must not be assumed acceptable. The current app’s output is not a certification of government compliance. Check the official photo requirements; retake an unsuitable original or use a professional service.

What about this website’s analytics?

This website uses the existing Cloudflare Web Analytics integration to measure page visits and performance. This is separate from image processing in either mobile app. These guide pages do not offer a photo-upload form, add an advertising tracker or set analytics cookies. See Cloudflare’s description of Web Analytics.
