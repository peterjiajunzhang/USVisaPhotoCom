Photo dimensions and framing measurements

See the pixel dimensions, file size, eye position and head height behind your preview. An app check is guidance, not official photo approval.

  • Print size — 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm), square crop.
  • Digital resolution — square side between 600 and 1200 px; the app shows your exact export size (e.g. 1200 × 1200).
  • Eye position — from the bottom of the frame, U.S. guidance targets 56–69%; we display your measured percentage.
  • Head height — 50–69% of the image height; we show your measured value against the template.
  • Background — a white background is generated by processing; this does not verify the original background.
  • Lighting — uneven facial lighting can trigger a warning; the app does not correct it.
  • File format — a JPEG image export; no multi-photo print layout is included.
  • Processing scope — cropping and background replacement; no manual retouching or crop-only export.

An app checklist or “Ready to use” label is not official approval. Follow the Department of State photo requirements, including the rules about an appropriate original and digital alterations.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose iPhone or Android?

Yes. Use either store link on any device. The iPhone version needs iOS 17 or later; the Android version needs Android 8.0 or later. Choose your store.

What is free, and what do I pay for?

Photo processing, preview and retries are free. Each purchase unlocks one JPEG photo export, with no subscription. The U.S. store price is $1.99 per export on each platform. Your store shows the current local price before purchase.

Does my purchase work on both platforms?

No. App Store and Google Play purchases and export credits are separate. Purchase on the platform where you will export your photo.

Are photos processed locally?

Yes. Portrait processing runs on your device. Android Google SDKs may send usage and performance diagnostics, and each store handles purchase data. See the iPhone policy or Android policy for the full details.

Does an export guarantee my photo will be accepted?

No. Both apps replace the background and do not offer a crop-only export. Official applications can restrict digitally edited photos. Check the receiving authority’s instructions. This independent app is not affiliated with a government or embassy. Read the editing limits.
