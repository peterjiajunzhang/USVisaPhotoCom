ANDROID QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is the Android app available to download?

Yes. US Visa Photo is available on Google Play, from Loop Apps. You do not need a closed-test invitation. Availability can depend on your device, region and Google account.

What can I do for free?

Photo processing, watermarked preview and retries are free. Purchase one JPEG export only if you choose to unlock the result. This is not a subscription or an unlimited-use pass.

Can I use an iPhone purchase on Android?

No. Purchases and export credits are separate between the App Store and Google Play. Choose the store for the device where you want to export.

Does the app upload my photo?

Image processing runs on your Android device, without a photo-processing upload to us. Google SDK diagnostic data and Google Play purchase records are separate from your portraits. Read the full Android policy.

Can it fix lighting or make a print sheet?

No. The app can warn about uneven facial lighting, but does not correct it. It exports one JPEG rather than a multi-photo print layout.

Will the photo be officially accepted?

Acceptance is decided by the receiving authority. This independent app is not affiliated with or endorsed by a government or embassy. Its output includes background replacement and must not be assumed acceptable for a government application.
